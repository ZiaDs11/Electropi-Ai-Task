# Loyalty Program (PiPoints)

You earn 1 PiPoint for every 10 EGP spent (excluding the delivery fee).
100 PiPoints can be redeemed for a 50 EGP discount on your next order.

Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Referring a friend gives you
150 bonus points once the friend completes their first order of 100 EGP or more.

PiPoints cannot be combined with promo codes on the same order. Tier status
(Silver at 500 lifetime points, Gold at 2000) unlocks priority support and
waived surge fees for Gold members.
