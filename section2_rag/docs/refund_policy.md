# Refund & Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is "preparing".
Once an order is "out for delivery" it can no longer be cancelled, but if it
arrives more than 20 minutes late you are entitled to a 50% refund of the
delivery fee.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.
Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded as wallet credit instead of cash.

Damaged or incorrect items must be reported within 24 hours of delivery with a
photo. Approved claims are refunded in full including the delivery fee.
