# Delivery Zones & Fees

We currently operate in Greater Cairo: Maadi, Nasr City, New Cairo, Heliopolis,
6th of October, Sheikh Zayed, and El Shorouk.

The base delivery fee is 25 EGP. A surge fee of 15 EGP applies during peak
hours (12:00-14:00 and 19:00-22:00) and during rain. Orders above 400 EGP get
free delivery outside surge hours.

Estimated delivery time is 30-45 minutes within a zone. Cross-zone delivery
(e.g. Maadi to 6th of October) is not supported; each restaurant only serves
its own zone.
