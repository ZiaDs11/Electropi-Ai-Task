# Courier & Support FAQ

You can contact your courier in-app once the order is "out for delivery".
If the courier can't reach you within 5 minutes of arrival, the order is
returned and a re-delivery fee of 25 EGP applies.

Tipping is optional and goes 100% to the courier. Tips can be added in-app up
to 24 hours after delivery.

For allergies, add a note at checkout; we forward it to the restaurant but
cannot guarantee an allergen-free kitchen. Contact support for urgent issues
via the Help section, available 9:00-23:00 daily.
